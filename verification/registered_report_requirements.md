# Registered Report Requirements — ICSME 2027 / EMSE

**Verified:** 22 November 2026  
**Source:** https://conf.researchr.org/track/icsme-2027/icsme-2027-registered-reports  
**Conference dates:** 7–11 September 2027, Toronto.

This note records what the live track page says. It does not infer a deadline or
a submission link that the page does not publish.

## 1. Track structure

- The track is run by *Empirical Software Engineering* (EMSE) in conjunction with
  ICSME.
- **Stage 1:** a report describing the planned study is reviewed by the RR track.
  Accepted studies may present at ICSME.
- **Stage 2:** after Stage 1 acceptance, the study is conducted and the full paper
  is submitted for review to **EMSE**. Results may be negative.

## 2. Outcome types

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In-Principal Acceptance (IPA)** | Design is relevant and sound; the author may run Stage 2. Publication follows if the protocol is followed or deviations are justified. |
| **Continuity Acceptance (CA)** | Design is relevant and methods appear appropriate, but exploratory implementation details may need follow-up checks. |
| **Rejection** | Design is not mature enough; comments are provided. |

**Only confirmatory studies are considered for IPA in ICSME 2027.** Exploratory
studies can be accepted, but not with IPA. This project's primary hypotheses
(H1 and H3) are confirmatory, so the IPA route is appropriate. H2 and H4 remain
exploratory and are not the basis of the IPA claim.

## 3. Format and review

| Requirement | Verified detail |
|---|---|
| Page limit | 6 pages plus 1 additional page of references; strict. |
| Format | Two-column IEEE conference proceedings; LaTeX `\documentclass[conference]{IEEEtran}`; Word US Letter template. |
| Review | Single-blind; author names and affiliations are visible. |
| Submission | PDF via EasyChair; **link TBA**. |
| Deadline | **TBA**. The conference important-dates page lists other tracks but not the RR track. |
| AI disclosure | Required by IEEE policy; identify the AI system and the sections that used it. |
| Deviations | All deviations must be documented in the Stage 2 paper. |
| Concurrent submission | The report must not be under review elsewhere. |

## 4. What this means for the project

- The Stage 1 outline's 6+1 page budget and IEEE two-column format are correct.
- Single-blind review means the real author name and “Independent Researcher” are
  used; no anonymization is needed.
- The accepted Stage 1 protocol is the registration. No OSF DOI is required for
  this route, and no retrospective OSF record should be created for the pilot.
- The deadline and EasyChair link cannot be filled in yet. They must be re-checked
  before submission; inventing either would be a factual error.
