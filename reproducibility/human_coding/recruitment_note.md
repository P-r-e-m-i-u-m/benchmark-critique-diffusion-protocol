## What to ask before they start

- Have you read the pilot coding table, the pilot summary, or the manuscript's
  reliability/results section?
- Have you discussed this project's scores with anyone?
- Do you have a conflict, e.g. are you an author of one of the 12 papers?

Record the answers in the return note, not in the CSV.

## What to ask when they return it

- Coder ID and completion date.
- Rough time spent.
- Conflicts of interest.
- Any tool use beyond search/reading, including LLM use.
- Any rubric wording that was confusing.

Keep those answers with the completed sheet. They are part of the audit trail.
